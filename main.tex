\section*{Research Experience}
\textbf{Research Assistant (REU)}, University of Michigan--Ann Arbor \hfill Summer 2024\\
\textit{Supervisor: Dr.~Austyn Simpson}\\
-- Produced the first \textit{two-parameter family} with varying Hilbert--Kunz multiplicities by combining Monsky/Trivedi density methods with new Segre-product constructions; \textbf{co-authored} resulting paper.\\
-- Initiated constructions toward lifting \textit{F}-purity \(\to\) perfectoid purity in mixed characteristic; \textbf{co-authored} a submission.\\
-- Implemented Macaulay2 routines for Segre and Veronese presentations; groundwork for a future package release.

\vspace{0.12cm}
\noindent\textbf{Research Assistant (REU)}, University of Wisconsin--Madison \hfill Summer 2022\\
\textit{Supervisor: Dr.~Betsy Stovall}\\
-- Computed Newton polygons associated to vector fields in \(\mathbb{R}^n\) for \(n\le 14\).\\
-- Developed combinatorial arguments giving bounds in all dimensions; presented results in three venues; \textbf{co-authored} a forthcoming paper.

\vspace{0.12cm}
\noindent\textbf{Undergraduate Researcher}, Madison Experimental Mathematics Lab \hfill Fall 2022\\
\textit{Supervisor: Dr.~Feng Zhu}\\
-- Constructed explicit generators in \(\mathrm{PSL}(2,\mathbb{R})\) for isometry subgroups of hyperbolic surfaces.\\
-- Studied Teichmüller theory and presented a poster to students and faculty.
